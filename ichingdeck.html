i ching deck

an oracle deck visualizing the 64 I Ching hexagrams and their tarot correspondence

tags: i ching, divination, tarot, card deck

The I Ching is an ancient Chinese oracle that I was studying as a way to get closer to my heritage and also to share with others. I created a deck that visualized the I Ching and also started to loosely map how they could correspond to certain tarot cards, in a way to better understand and interpret the I Ching.

How to read the I Ching by Khanh Nguyen

The I Ching is an ancient Chinese oracle based off yin and yang. There are eight trigrams: earth, mountain, water, wind, thunder, fire, lake, heaven. These stack to create the 64 hexagrams. Hexagrams are built from the ground up.
